## Projects

- **Raineworks TV is no longer on Google Play.** Its TV app is installed by the Home Assistant integration when you
  set it up, so its page no longer offers Get it on Google Play or the beta. Its privacy policy is still linked there.
- **HA Slate has a page.** The firmware and Home Assistant integration for a PoE wall touch panel, with a kiosk
  dashboard, an on-device voice assistant with custom wake words and an edge light bar. Its documentation is here too.
