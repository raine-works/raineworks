## Look and feel

- **A new accent: peach into teal.** The site is still black and white; the two colours blend through the logo, the
  name on the home page, the thin lines under the header and over the footer, and the border of a card under your
  pointer. The favicon and the home-screen icon have it too.
- **The link preview** for raineworks.com has the new colours.

## Projects

- **Every project has a page of its own**, with what it is, what it does best, how to get started and how it works,
  beside its stars and what it’s built with on GitHub. Open one from its card, then go on to the next.
- **Project cards are tidier**: a few of what each is built with, and a count of the rest, so cards stay the same size.

## On phones

- **Tighter, easier pages**: the home page’s buttons share the width, hobbies sit beside their icons, and the work
  timeline gives its text more room.
