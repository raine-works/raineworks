## Everywhere

- **A new design.** The site is rebuilt on shadcn/ui: cleaner pages, the Geist typeface, and a **Theme** menu that follows your device or stays light or dark.
- **The raineworks logo** is in the header and footer, and on the browser tab.
- **On a phone**, the pages open from a menu that slides in from the side.
- **About** (the version in the footer) shows what’s new in each release and, under **Legal**, the site’s own licence and every open-source package it’s built with.

## Projects

- Every project card opens its repository on GitHub.
- New: **ha-grok** (xAI Grok for Home Assistant) and **ha-everscent** (EverScent diffusers for Home Assistant).
