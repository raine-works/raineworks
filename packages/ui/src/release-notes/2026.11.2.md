## ssh raineworks.com

- **A new terminal app.** `ssh raineworks.com` opens the site as a full-screen app: the logo over falling rain, then Work, Projects, Hobbies, Contact and About, with colour and the mouse.
- **Get around your way:** the arrow keys, number keys, a click, or **⌃K** to search for any page or action. **?** lists every key.
- **Send a message from the terminal.** Contact has a form that goes to the same inbox as the site’s.
- It fits your window: a sidebar when there’s room, tabs when there isn’t.
