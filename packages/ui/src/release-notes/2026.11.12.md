## Look and feel

- **Buttons that do the main thing glow softly** in peach and teal, a little brighter under your pointer: See my
  projects, Send message, View the source.
- **Cards are plain again until you point at one**, when the accent comes up in its border. Outlined buttons are back
  to black and white.
